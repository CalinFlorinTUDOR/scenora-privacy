# Scenora — Account & Data Deletion

**Last updated: November 7, 2026**

Scenora is a personal memory organizer developed by **KaliDevi Studio**.

This page explains how users can request deletion of their Scenora account and associated personal data.

## 1. Delete Your Account Within Scenora

You can delete your Scenora account directly from the application.

Follow these steps:

1. Open Scenora.
2. Sign in to your account.
3. Open the main menu (☰).
4. Select **My Profile**.
5. Select **Delete Account**.
6. Confirm your decision.
7. Complete identity verification if requested.

Account deletion is permanent.

## 2. Request Account Deletion Without the App

If you cannot access Scenora, you can request account deletion by contacting KaliDevi Studio.

**Email:** kalidevistudio@gmail.com

**Suggested email subject:** Scenora — Account Deletion Request

In your message, provide the email address associated with your Scenora account and state that you wish to delete your account.

For security reasons, additional identity verification may be required.

**Never send your password or payment card information.**

## 3. What Data Is Deleted?

When your account is successfully deleted, Scenora removes the associated Firebase Authentication account and application-managed account information stored in Cloud Firestore.

Locally stored memories, photographs, notes, places, and trips may also be removed from the device during the deletion process.

If you wish to remove remaining local application data, you can clear Scenora's application storage through your Android device settings or uninstall the application.

## 4. Data That May Be Retained

Certain information may be retained when required for legal, financial, security, or fraud-prevention purposes.

Google Play and other service providers may retain transaction records according to their own retention policies and applicable laws.

KaliDevi Studio does not use deleted account information for advertising.

## 5. Google Play Subscriptions

**Deleting your Scenora account does not automatically cancel an active Google Play subscription.**

To cancel your subscription:

1. Open Google Play.
2. Select your profile.
3. Open **Payments & subscriptions**.
4. Select **Subscriptions**.
5. Choose **Scenora**.
6. Select **Cancel subscription**.

Subscription management and billing are handled by Google Play.

## 6. Processing Your Request

KaliDevi Studio will review account deletion requests and may request additional information to verify account ownership.

We aim to respond within 30 days of receiving a valid request, subject to applicable legal requirements.

If additional time is legally permitted and necessary, we will inform you.
