# Privacy Policy — Scenora

**Last updated: September 12, 2026**

KaliDevi Studio built Scenora as a personal memory organizer. This Privacy Policy explains how Scenora handles information when you use the application.

## Information Scenora Uses

Scenora allows users to create and organize memories, places, photos, and trips.

Depending on the features you choose to use, Scenora may access:

- **Location information** to associate memories, photos, or trips with a location.
- **Photos and images** that you select from your device or capture for use in Scenora.
- **Text contained in images** through on-device optical character recognition (OCR).
- **Image characteristics and labels** generated through on-device image analysis.
- **Information you enter manually**, such as place names, trip names, notes, and memories.

## Location

Scenora may request access to your device's approximate or precise location.

Location is used to help associate saved content with a place and to display useful location information, such as a city or country.

Location access is only used when required by Scenora's location-related features. You can control location permissions through your device settings.

## Photos, Camera and Image Processing

Scenora allows you to select images from your device and to capture photos.

Images may be processed using text recognition and image-labeling technology so that Scenora can identify text or useful characteristics within an image and make saved memories easier to find.

Scenora does not use your photos for advertising.

## Data Storage and Processing

Scenora is designed primarily as a device-based personal memory organizer.

Memory information created by the user, including notes and references to saved images, is stored locally on the user's device using application storage.

Scenora uses on-device machine-learning functionality for features such as text recognition and image labeling.

Scenora does not currently provide a KaliDevi Studio account or cloud synchronization service, and KaliDevi Studio does not operate a server that receives your Scenora memories, photos, OCR results, or location history.

Some operating-system or third-party components used by the application may process information as necessary to provide their functionality and may be governed by their own privacy practices.

## Data Sharing

KaliDevi Studio does not sell your personal information.

Scenora does not share your saved memories, photos, notes, OCR results, or location history with KaliDevi Studio for advertising or marketing purposes.

## Data Deletion

You can delete individual memories, photos, places, and trips from within Scenora.

You can also remove locally stored application data by uninstalling Scenora or by clearing the application's storage through your device settings.

## Permissions

Scenora may request device permissions necessary for features selected by the user, including location and access required to select or capture images.

You may deny or revoke permissions through your device settings. Some Scenora features may not function without the permissions they require.

## Children's Privacy

Scenora is not specifically designed for children. KaliDevi Studio does not knowingly operate a service intended to collect personal information from children.

## Security

Scenora is designed to minimize external collection of personal information by keeping its core memory data on the user's device.

However, no method of electronic storage or processing can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Scenora's features or data practices change.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
