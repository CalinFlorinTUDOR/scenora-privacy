# Privacy Policy — Scenora

**Last updated: November 7, 2026**

Scenora is a personal memory organizer developed by KaliDevi Studio. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and protected when you use Scenora.

## 1. Information We Process

Depending on the features you use, Scenora may process the following information:

- **Account information:** Your email address, authentication identifiers, and optional profile information.
- **Authentication information:** Information required to sign in using email and password or Google Sign-In.
- **Location information:** Approximate or precise location used to associate memories, photos, and trips with places.
- **Photos and images:** Images selected from your device or captured using its camera.
- **Image analysis information:** Text recognized through OCR and labels generated through on-device image analysis.
- **User-created content:** Memories, notes, place names, trip information, and other information you choose to save.
- **Subscription information:** Subscription status, purchase verification information, trial start date, and access entitlements.

## 2. Account Registration and Authentication

Scenora supports account registration and authentication through Firebase Authentication, a service provided by Google.

Users may sign in using an email address and password or Google Sign-In.

When Google Sign-In is used, Scenora receives authentication information necessary to identify the user and provide access to the application.

Authentication credentials are handled through Firebase Authentication and the relevant authentication provider.

## 3. Location Information

Scenora may request access to approximate or precise device location.

Location information is used to associate saved memories, photographs, and trips with geographical locations.

Users can control location permissions through their device settings.

Location information associated with memories is stored locally as part of the application's personal memory data.

## 4. Photos, Camera, and Image Processing

Scenora allows users to select images from their devices or capture photographs.

Images may be processed using on-device optical character recognition and image-labeling technology.

These features help users organize and search their saved memories.

Scenora does not use personal photographs for advertising.

## 5. Local Data Storage

Scenora stores personal memory content primarily on the user's device.

This includes saved memories, notes, image references, OCR results, image labels, and associated location information.

Scenora does not currently provide cloud synchronization or cloud backup of personal memory collections.

Uninstalling the application or clearing its storage may permanently remove locally stored content.

## 6. Firebase and Cloud Services

Scenora uses Firebase services provided by Google to support account authentication, subscription access, and application functionality.

Cloud Firestore may store information such as:

- User authentication identifiers.
- Trial start dates.
- Lifetime PRO access status.
- Subscription status and expiration information.
- Subscription verification timestamps.
- Optional account profile information.

Firebase Cloud Functions are used to verify subscription information and manage access entitlements.

Personal memory collections and photographs are not intentionally uploaded to KaliDevi Studio's Firebase database as part of Scenora's current functionality.

Google's services may process technical information necessary for authentication, security, and service operation.

## 7. Free Trial and Scenora PRO Subscriptions

Scenora provides a 30-day free trial, subject to the application's eligibility rules.

Trial eligibility and access rights are associated with the user's account.

After the trial expires, users without an active subscription may retain read-only access to previously saved content, while creating or modifying content requires Scenora PRO.

Scenora PRO subscriptions are processed through Google Play Billing.

KaliDevi Studio does not directly collect or store users' complete payment card details.

Subscription-related information may be processed to verify purchases, determine access rights, prevent subscription misuse, and provide customer support.

Deleting a Scenora account does not automatically cancel an active Google Play subscription. Users must manage or cancel subscriptions separately through Google Play.

## 8. How Information Is Used

Information is processed to:

- Provide and maintain Scenora's functionality.
- Authenticate users and manage accounts.
- Organize and search personal memories.
- Associate saved content with locations.
- Verify subscription purchases and manage PRO access.
- Support account security and prevent misuse.
- Respond to user requests and support inquiries.

Where applicable, processing is based on providing the requested service, legitimate interests in security and service integrity, legal obligations, or user consent for optional device permissions.

## 9. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

KaliDevi Studio does not sell personal information.

Scenora uses third-party services, including:

- Google Firebase Authentication.
- Google Cloud Firestore.
- Firebase Cloud Functions.
- Google Sign-In.
- Google Play Billing.
- On-device image recognition and labeling technologies.

These providers may process information necessary to deliver their services under their respective privacy policies.

Scenora does not share personal memories, photographs, notes, OCR results, or location history with advertisers for targeted advertising.

## 10. Data Retention

Locally stored memories and associated information remain on the user's device until deleted, cleared, or removed through application management.

Account-related information stored in Firebase is maintained while necessary to provide account functionality and subscription access.

When an account is deleted, Scenora initiates deletion of the associated account information and locally managed application data, subject to technical limitations and any applicable legal retention requirements.

Certain transaction records may remain with Google Play or relevant service providers according to their retention policies and applicable law.

## 11. Account and Data Deletion

Users can delete individual memories, photographs, places, and trips from within Scenora.

Users can also request account deletion through:

**Scenora → My Profile → Delete Account**

Account deletion requires confirmation and may require reauthentication to protect against unauthorized requests.

Deleting a Scenora account removes its associated authentication account and application-managed account information, subject to applicable retention requirements.

Locally stored application data may also be removed during the deletion process.

Users who cannot access the application may contact KaliDevi Studio to request assistance with account or personal data deletion.

**Contact:** kalidevistudio@gmail.com

Google Play subscriptions must be canceled separately.

## 12. Permissions

Scenora may request permissions for:

- Camera access.
- Photo or media selection.
- Approximate or precise location.

Permissions are requested when necessary for selected application features.

Users can deny or revoke permissions through Android device settings.

Some functionality may become unavailable without the required permissions.

## 13. Children's Privacy

Scenora is not specifically designed for children.

KaliDevi Studio does not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

If a parent or guardian believes that a child's personal information has been collected improperly, they may contact KaliDevi Studio to request appropriate action.

## 14. Data Security

Scenora uses Firebase authentication and access controls to help protect account information.

Personal memory content is primarily stored locally on the user's device.

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect information processed by the application.

However, no electronic storage or transmission system can guarantee absolute security.

## 15. International Data Processing

Scenora uses Google services that may process account-related information in countries outside the user's country of residence.

Such processing is subject to applicable data protection requirements and the safeguards implemented by the relevant service providers.

## 16. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, users may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of their personal information.

Users may also have rights to object to certain processing activities, request data portability, or withdraw consent where consent is the applicable legal basis.

Users in the European Economic Area may also have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent data protection authority.

To submit a privacy-related request, contact KaliDevi Studio.
